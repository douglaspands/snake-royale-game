## Why
To optimize playability, tactical awareness, and visual clarity across desktop and mobile screens, the in-game HUD layout requires ergonomic redistribution into distinct screen corners: Top-Right for Leaderboard, Bottom-Left for Vital Stats, and Bottom-Right for Radar Minimap.

## What Changes
- Ergonomic 3-corner HUD layout redistribution.
- Unified compact player vital stats displaying Score and Rank.
- Bottom-right radar minimap positioning and mobile turbo button clearance.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `hud`: Three-corner ergonomic HUD layout, vital statistics, and bottom-right radar minimap.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact
- Frontend HUD and renderer updated (`client/src/ui/hud.ts`, `client/src/render/renderer.ts`, `client/src/index.css`).
